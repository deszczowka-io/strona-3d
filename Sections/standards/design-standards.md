# Design Standards

## Creative bar
Award-level (Awwwards/FWA/Codrops) creative websites — not generic AI-generated landing pages or templates.

## Principles
- **Typography first.** Establish the type scale before color or layout.
- **Intentional whitespace.** Premium feel comes from restraint, not density.
- **Motion with purpose.** Every animation serves hierarchy or storytelling.
- **No template layouts.** Avoid centered hero + three feature cards + CTA unless the brief calls for it.

## Deliverables
- `visual-system.md` — color palette, type scale, spacing, motion character.
- `layout-direction.md` — composition philosophy.
- `design-spec.md` — page-by-page visual treatment.

## Review
Creative-director approves all visual deliverables before implementation builds against them.
